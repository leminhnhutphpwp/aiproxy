# Agile & Scrum Practices

## Core Principles
- **Iterative Delivery**: Ship working software every sprint (1-4 weeks), gather feedback, adapt
- **Inspect & Adapt**: Retrospectives drive continuous process improvement
- **Empirical Process**: Transparency, inspection, and adaptation replace rigid plans
- **Self-Organizing Teams**: Team decides how to do the work, not commanded from above

## Key Patterns
- **Timeboxing**: Fixed sprint length — scope flexes, not time
- **Daily Standup**: 15-min sync — what I did, what I'll do, blockers
- **Sprint Planning**: Team commits to work items for the sprint (capacity-based)
- **Sprint Review**: Demo working software to stakeholders, collect feedback
- **Retrospective**: Blameless analysis — what went well, what to improve, action items
- **Backlog Refinement**: Regularly groom and estimate PBIs for upcoming sprints

## Best Practices
- Keep stories INVEST (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable)
- Use relative estimation (story points), not hours — team calibrates together
- Limit WIP — finish started work before pulling new items
- Definition of Done must be explicit and visible to everyone
- Sprint Goal gives purpose — don't just deliver tickets, deliver outcomes
- Retro action items must have owners and be tracked to completion
- PO is the single voice of the customer — avoid scope creep mid-sprint

## Common Pitfalls
- **Zombie Scrum**: Doing ceremonies without the mindset — sprint but no adaptation
- **Estimation inflation**: Points creep up over time — recalibrate with reference stories
- **Too many stories in progress**: Multitasking kills throughput — enforce WIP limits
- **Retro without change**: Retros are useless if no action items are implemented
- **PO absence**: Without an engaged PO, the team builds the wrong thing
- **Fixed-scope sprints**: Treating sprint as a deadline destroys agility — scope flexes

## Tools & Techniques
- **Planning Poker**: Consensus-based estimation (modified Fibonacci)
- **Kanban Board**: Visualize flow (Todo → In Progress → Review → Done)
- **Burndown/Burnup Charts**: Track sprint progress and scope changes
- **Velocity Tracking**: Measure throughput over time for reliable forecasting
- **Cycle Time / Lead Time**: Measure process efficiency, not output
- **Sprint Health Check**: Team sentiment survey every sprint
